The three numbers that move the list

Net support
Endorse minus oppose

Simple and familiar. It sorts the Top tab. Delegated votes count once, and your own vote always overrides a delegate's.

Common ground
Only as strong as the weakest group

We group members by how they vote, never by party. A priority's score is its support in the group that likes it least, on a 0 to 100 scale. One side alone can't push it up.

Momentum
Places gained this week

Sorts the Rising tab. Sudden spikes from new or linked accounts are held for review before they count.
