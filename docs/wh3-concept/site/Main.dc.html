How it works

Four steps from idea to agenda

1
Verify once

Confirm you are one real U.S. resident. Your name stays private unless you choose to show it.

2
Endorse or oppose

Back the priorities you want and push against the ones you don't. Change your mind any time.

3
Find common ground

Raw volume can be gamed. The common-ground score rewards priorities that people who usually disagree both support.

4
Hand it to officials

Each week the top of the list goes out as an open snapshot. Compare it with where your officials stand.

From 2008 to now

What White House 2 got right, and what we changed

In 2008 Jim Gilliam built White House 2 to imagine a White House run by thousands of people online. The core idea holds up. The tools around it needed an update.
